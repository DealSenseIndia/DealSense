## The interesting part: price is not linear in size

The 4.2-litre Milton is ₹323 **cheaper** than the 3.5-litre Borosil, and ₹604 cheaper than the 3.3-litre Pigeon. Capacity does not decide price nearly as much as stock, model age and discount cycles do.

That means the honest question is not "which air fryer is best" but:

1. **How much food do you actually cook at once?** A 3-litre basket is tight for four people. Two people, it is plenty.
2. **What is the wattage?** 1200–1500 W is the normal band. A 1200 W unit will take longer than a 1450 W one for the same load; that is physics, not marketing.
3. **Is today's price below this model's own median?** That is the only question that changes the answer from "this is the right size" to "this is the right day".

## What we do not know

We do not cook in these machines. We cannot tell you whether the basket coating lasts, whether the fan is loud, or whether the digital panel is pleasant to use. Those are review questions and we do not fabricate answers to them.
